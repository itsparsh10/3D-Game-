1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & BRAND IDENTITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Project: "Gloop's Glitch Lab"
• Tagline: "Run. Fail. Learn. Again." / "One challenge. One run. Thirty seconds."
• Concept: A 3D obstacle-runner browser game engineered as an automated video 
  factory. A user/creator inputs or selects a challenge, the engine generates 
  a deterministic seeded level, executes a high-stakes run, composites text, 
  scores, dynamic overlays and audio, and exports a vertical YouTube Short 
  (1080x1920, 9:16, 30s, 30fps, H.264 MP4).
• Core Metaphor: The audience watches a small hero tackle a giant, absurd 
  course, gather rewards, grow, dodge lethal traps, navigate an unexpected 
  twist, and reach an unambiguous win/fail resolution.

2. CORE CHARACTERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Gloop (The Hero)
   - Visual: Rounded glossy turquoise/cyan jelly blob, oversized expressive eyes
     (white sclera, dark pupils, shiny catchlights), readable eyebrows, cute 
     mouth, stubby jelly arms, and orange-and-white sneakers.
   - 6 Expression States: Happy, Surprised, Determined, Scared, Sad, Win.
   - Dynamic Growth Mechanic: As Gloop collects orbs, he grows! Collected orbs 
     form a trailing stack/chain of glossy spheres under him, elevating him 
     higher. On the victory screen, Gloop stands crowned atop the orb tower.
   - Animation States: Idle breathing squash, run cycle with shoe kicks and arm 
     pumps, high stretch jump, flat slide squash, lane tilt, hit-react, victory.

B. Glitchmaker (The Antagonist)
   - Visual: Menacing floating purple glowing cube with angry orange/yellow eye slits
     and orbiting voxel/pixel glitch shards.
   - 4 States: Normal, Angry, Glitch, Laugh.
   - Role: Spawns during Beat 3 (Twist) to trigger rule flips, gravity shifts,
     or projectile barrages.

C. Pip (The Guide)
   - Visual: Cute floating golden star guide offering navigation hints and audio 
     accents during key moments.

3. THE FOUR WORLDS (Distinct Visual Art Direction)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Each world features unique palettes, fog, props, lighting, and materials:
1. Sky Islands:
   - Floating green emerald islands, bright cyan sky, fluffy volumetric clouds, 
     floating fantasy castles, waterfalls, and golden finish portal.
2. Candy Factory:
   - Pastel pink roadways, oversized striped gumdrop mounds, candy cane arches, 
     frosting curbs, and jelly obstacles.
3. Ice World:
   - Glacial frosted tracks, deep blue sapphire ice crystal spires, falling snow 
     particles, and reflective ice sheen.
4. Lava Land:
   - Volcanic dark basalt/obsidian bridges, churning glowing magma below, 
     fiery orange fissure lighting, and floating ember particles.

4. CORE RULES & MECHANICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• Objective: Reach the golden "FINAL TEST" arch with >= Target Orbs before 
  the timer reaches 0.
• Orb Rules: Blue orbs = +1; Red orbs = -1 / hazard. A mid-run "RULE FLIP" twist 
  swaps the colors (Red becomes good, Blue bad) with immediate HUD notification.
• Mathematical Choice Gates: Green is ALWAYS positive (+5 Orbs); Red is ALWAYS 
  negative (-3 Orbs).
• Hazards:
  - Giant swinging red-and-white pendulum hammers (duck or switch lane).
  - Ground-based rotating spike cylinders (jump over).
  - Spring-loaded bounce pads (super launch over hazards into orb arcs).
• Heart / Life System: Configurable per episode (e.g., Ep 002 is strictly 
  "One hit ends the run").
• Controller: 3 lanes (Left: -2.4, Center: 0, Right: +2.4), Jump, Slide.
  Inputs supported: Keyboard (A/D/Space/S/Arrows), Touch/Swipe, and Bot Autopilot.

5. THE 30-SECOND NARRATIVE TIMELINE (6 BEATS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
• 00–03s | Beat 1 — HOOK: Immediate tension, prominent goal banner ("20 ORBS? ONE RUN.")
• 03–08s | Beat 2 — COLLECT & GROW: Fast orb trails, chime combos, stack begins growing
• 08–14s | Beat 3 — TWIST: Rule flip, color gate choice, or Glitchmaker appearance
• 14–22s | Beat 4 — HARD OBSTACLES: Swinging hammers, spike rollers, tight timing
• 22–28s | Beat 5 — FINAL RUN: Final sprint, finish arch looms, orb count closing in
• 28–30s | Beat 6 — RESULT: Golden arch crossed; WIN fanfare + confetti or FAIL cue

7. TECHNICAL ARCHITECTURE: 3-TIER SEPARATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
RULE: The simulation is strictly isolated from visual rendering.
Visual polish must never alter collision math, physics updates, RNG seeding, or replay.

   +-------------------------------------------------------------+
   | 1. EPISODE CONFIG (Data Layer)                              |
   |    - Pure JSON/TS config: world, target, hearts, duration,  |
   |      seed, and sequence of narrative beat events.           |
   +------------------------------+------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
   +-------------------------------------------------------------+
   | 2. SIMULATION ENGINE (Deterministic Logic)                  |
   |    - 60Hz fixed-step runner physics, lane math, collision   |
   |      volumes, orb counts, beat transitions, seeded RNG.    |
   +------------------------------+------------------------------+
                                  |
                                  v
   +-------------------------------------------------------------+
   | 3. VIEW & COMPOSITING LAYER (Three.js & Remotion)           |
   |    - R3F Canvas, stylized shaders, GLB meshes, lighting,    |
   |      chase camera, HUD, Web Audio synth, Remotion exporter. |
   +-------------------------------------------------------------+

Identified Visual & Mechanical Improvements (The Quality Bar):
  1. Gloop Face in Gameplay: Gloop is currently viewed mostly from behind. 
     Add a dynamic camera angle or slight 3/4 chase offset so his expressive 
     face, eyes, and moods are clearly readable in the hook and victory shots.
  2. Trailing Orb Stack: Replace uniform scaling with the canonical chain 
     of collected spheres stacking beneath Gloop.
  3. Stumble Guys Material Polish: Upgrade track from solid color to glossy 
     checkerboard with toy-like striped side curbs, soft ambient occlusion, 
     and bloom highlights.
  4. Curated GLB Meshes: Transition from procedural CSG primitives to low-poly 
     curated GLB models for castles, hammers, and character meshes as specified 
     in IMPLEMENTATION.md.
  5. Guaranteed Bot Win Margin: In "Record Mode", calibrate the bot controller 
     to reliably reach >= Target Orbs with a 1-2 orb safety buffer for flawless 
     video exports every single time.
